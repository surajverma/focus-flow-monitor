    YOU ARE IN CONTROL

    Tracking Settings

    Choose how long without mouse or keyboard activity before tracking pauses. Longer times help track passive viewing (like videos) but may inflate totals if you walk away. The default is 30 minutes. "Never" disables idle checking.

    Automatically delete daily tracking data older than the selected period. Setting a shorter duration can help maintain performance over time. "Forever" keeps all data. (Pruning happens approximately once per day).

        How is my focus score calculated?

        Productive time ÷ all tracked time × 100. Category ratings come from your Productivity settings. Neutral and distracting time count toward the total. Excluded websites are omitted. Best day is the day with the highest focus score; ties use tracked time. Reports and goals use retained browsing history, so deletion or retention cleanup can change the results.
